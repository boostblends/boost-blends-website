E98 ethanol: common questions

How much is E98 ethanol?

E98 is $5.20 per gallon for 15 to 54 gallons and $275 per 55-gallon drum. Three or more drums are $4.85 per gallon, and 275-gallon totes are quoted individually. Check the homepage for current promotions.

What is E98 used for?

E98 is high-concentration ethanol that racers and tuners use to mix their own E85 or a custom ethanol blend for flex-fuel and performance builds.

Can I blend my own E85 with E98?

Yes. Many tuners buy E98 by the drum or tote and mix it with gasoline to hit the ethanol content their tune calls for. Check your vehicle's fuel requirements first.

Do you deliver E98?

Yes. Full 55-gallon E98 drums and 275-gallon totes can be delivered around Portland, Salem and Vancouver, WA. Jug fill-ups are pickup only.

What is the minimum E98 order?

The minimum is 15 gallons at an arranged pickup. Delivery starts at one full 55-gallon drum.
